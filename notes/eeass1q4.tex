Question Four
i. Find the intrinsic carrier concentration ($n_i$) in silicon at $300\text{ K}$
The formula for the intrinsic carrier concentration is given by:
$$n_i = N \cdot e^{-\frac{E_g}{2kT}}$$ 
Given parameters:
$N = 3 \times 10^{25}\text{ m}^{-3}$
$E_g = 1.1\text{ eV}$
$k = 8.62 \times 10^{-5}\text{ eV/K}$
$T = 300\text{ K}$
Calculation:
Compute the thermal denominator energy term:
$$2kT = 2 \times (8.62 \times 10^{-5}\text{ eV/K}) \times 300\text{ K} = 0.05172\text{ eV}$$ 
Compute the exponent:
$$-\frac{E_g}{2kT} = -\frac{1.1\text{ eV}}{0.05172\text{ eV}} \approx -21.2684$$ 
Compute the exponential factor:
$$e^{-21.2684} \approx 5.798 \times 10^{-10}$$ 
Solve for $n_i$:
$$n_i = (3 \times 10^{25}\text{ m}^{-3}) \times (5.798 \times 10^{-10}) = \mathbf{1.74 \times 10^{16}\text{ m}^{-3}}$$ 
ii. Determine the conductivity ($\sigma$) of silicon
The electrical conductivity for an intrinsic semiconductor is:
$$\sigma = q \cdot n_i \cdot (\mu_e + \mu_h)$$ 
Given parameters:
$q = 1.6 \times 10^{-19}\text{ C}$
$n_i = 1.74 \times 10^{16}\text{ m}^{-3}$
$\mu_e = 0.15\text{ m}^2/\text{V}\cdot\text{s}$
$\mu_h = 0.06\text{ m}^2/\text{V}\cdot\text{s}$
Calculation:
$$\mu_e + \mu_h = 0.15 + 0.06 = 0.21\text{ m}^2/\text{V}\cdot\text{s}$$ 
$$\sigma = (1.6 \times 10^{-19}) \times (1.74 \times 10^{16}) \times 0.21 = \mathbf{5.85 \times 10^{-4}\text{ S/m}}\ \text{(or }\Omega^{-1}\cdot\text{m}^{-1}\text{)}$$ 
iii. Find the energy gap ($E_g$) of the semiconductor material
Rearranging the carrier concentration equation to solve for $E_g$:
$$E_g = 2kT \cdot \ln\left(\frac{N}{n_i}\right)$$ 
Given parameters:
$T = 30\ ^\circ\text{C} = 30 + 273.15 = 303.15\text{ K}$
$n_i = 1.60 \times 10^{16}\text{ m}^{-3}$
$N = 3 \times 10^{25}\text{ m}^{-3}$
$k = 8.62 \times 10^{-5}\text{ eV/K}$
Calculation:
Compute the ratio:
$$\frac{N}{n_i} = \frac{3 \times 10^{25}}{1.60 \times 10^{16}} = 1.875 \times 10^9$$ 
Compute the natural logarithm:
$$\ln(1.875 \times 10^9) \approx 21.3519$$ 
Compute the thermal energy term:
$$2kT = 2 \times (8.62 \times 10^{-5}) \times 303.15 = 0.05226\text{ eV}$$ 
Solve for $E_g$:
$$E_g = 0.05226 \times 21.3519 = \mathbf{1.12\text{ eV}}$$
